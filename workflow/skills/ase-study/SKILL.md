# Automotive study workflow

Read the active course's context and existing agent instructions before changing files. Continue the user's requested mode: framework design, note intake, research, study, or final synthesis. Existing authorization and the latest user direction govern; do not turn “not ready for the final guide” into a research prohibition, or “we will start next term” into permission to start now.

## Essential decisions

- Class-final preparation follows instructor coverage, supplied textbook material, notes, and shop examples. ASE preparation uses the whole relevant directory plus the verified official exam task list. The two outputs can share explanations while retaining different scope.
- Preserve raw notes and existing guides. Put external qualifications in research or clearly labeled guide additions. Ongoing notes are incomplete evidence, not proof of a knowledge gap.
- Inventory and read relevant contents before synthesis. A filename or another assistant's summary is not equivalent to reading the source. Record unavailable material and still complete useful independent work.
- If personal notes are sparse or absent and research is requested, use a web-first entry: find public notes, lessons, diagrams, and examples; map their coverage and verify important claims. Do not require the learner to supply notes before authorized web research can begin, or invent instructor scope from another course's notes.
- Distinguish classroom statements, documented facts, reasoned inference, and open questions. Keep application-specific specifications attached to their identified assembly and source.
- Use official weights to account for coverage; adapt study effort to the learner's experience and observed results. Do not invent subtopic frequency or a probability of passing.
- Lead a study guide with the broad exam-ready classification or mechanism. Keep service-manual depth, product-specific sequences, and evidence qualifications in the research layer unless the instructor or question explicitly names that design. Detail should support recall, not bury it.
- When notes change, compare the new material with the current guides and update only affected sections. Propagate it differently to a unit guide, class-final guide, and ASE guide according to each output's scope. Preserve stable filenames when useful and put a visible version/date on rendered copies that may coexist.
- Treat supplied or owned commercial and manufacturer question banks as separate practice resources. Do not reproduce their questions or replace them with a large invented bank. Create original practice only when the user asks, when instructor-style practice is part of the deliverable, or when a specific uncovered weakness needs a new variation.
- Either assistant can draft or review. Keep roles flexible, preserve handoff state in files, and avoid concurrent edits to the same document.
- Use the existing structure. Prefer one combined research file, a context file, and guide/cram-sheet pairs created as needed. Do not create a framework's entire optional directory tree in an established notes folder.

If the user requests a new course folder, use `scripts/new_course.py DESTINATION --exam A2` (or another supported exam), or adapt the templates manually. The script creates empty study scaffolding only and refuses existing destinations. Do not run it merely because a future course is mentioned. Ordinary notes and study material do not require a repository, scheduler, cloud service, or new assistant task.

At handoff, record the absolute date, actual files reviewed, output versions changed, unresolved issue, and next useful action. Keep the user's private materials local unless they explicitly authorize sharing the specific contents; publishing this reusable framework does not authorize publishing their course folder.
