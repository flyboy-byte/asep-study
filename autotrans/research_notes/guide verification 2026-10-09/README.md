# Guide verification, 2026-10-09

A small full-page check of the highest-risk claims in `study/ase/ase_a2_study_guide.md` v1. Four local agents each took one part of the guide and were cut to the 8-15 riskiest claims. All pages were read through WebFetch (it summarizes the page, so excerpts are the tool's quoted text). Some sites returned 403 or did not resolve (Underhood Service, ALLDATA, Transmission Digest).

Files: `verify_sec1_6_7.md`, `verify_sec2.md`, `verify_sec3.md`, `verify_sec45.md`. Each lists CORRECTED/UNVERIFIABLE items first, then confirmed claims, then what was NOT checked.

Result (about 53 claims): most confirmed from full pages. Applied to guide v1.1: Simpson and LePelletier wording; seized-stator "normal stall" softened to reasoned/not diagnostic; reverse-boost reason marked INFERRED; 80-warm-up rule attributed to OEM convention (CARB text gives 40); permanent DTC phase-in 2010-2012; level-check trap now covers topping off cold; cooler breach and glycol wording sourced; drum criteria limited to what a transmission source supports; unsupported "hardest area" claim removed.

Still unverified (kept in the guide as written, not contradicted): CAN checks, voltage-drop limits, Hall vs. magnetic testing, solenoid current ramp, scan-tool bidirectional details, adapt-reading rules, pan-debris table, TR switch checks, friction soak time, drum reject limits, TBV behavior, valve-role tables.
