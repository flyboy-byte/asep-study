## 7. Excluded: brain dumps and sellers of "actual" questions

### Takeaway
Avoid any site selling "actual," "real" or "leaked" ASE questions, or "dumps." That is cheating under the certification's security rules and is often wrong. The dump sites found in results mostly target unrelated "ASE" certifications (HPE ASE, SAFe ASE), which adds to the confusion.

### Cited Findings
- DOCUMENTED (snippet): The results included dump sellers with "ASE" in their listings, such as certkillers.net ("ASE Brain Dumps"), marks4sure.com ("ASE Certification Practice Test Dumps… 100% Guaranteed"), dumpsarena.com and free-braindumps.com. Several are for HPE "ASE" or SAFe-ASE, not automotive ASE. **EXCLUDED — do not use.** — [certkillers (excluded)](https://www.certkillers.net/Certification/ASE); [marks4sure (excluded)](https://www.marks4sure.com/ASE-cert.html); [dumpsarena (excluded, HPE ASE)](https://dumpsarena.com/hp-certification/ase/)
- DOCUMENTED (snippet, analogous certification body): CompTIA warns that brain dumps are cheating, can lead to bans and revoked certifications, and are often inaccurate. It also says operators hire test-takers to harvest items. — [CompTIA: Why brain dumps don't work](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/blog/why-brain-dumps-dont-work/). This is CompTIA policy, not ASE policy.
- OPEN: ASE's own confidentiality/misconduct wording (on the test-center policies page) couldn't be fetched. — [ASE Test Center Policies](https://ase.com/test-center-policies/)

### Inferences
- INFERRED: Red flags: "actual exam questions," "real test questions," "guaranteed pass," "dumps," or "VCE/PDF of the real exam." Legitimate sources describe their items as "ASE-style" or "practice."
