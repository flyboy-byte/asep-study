# research_notes/

Raw research evidence for A2, one folder per research pass. These files are **inputs to curate**, not study guides. The curated coverage record is `../research.md`, which indexes each file with its task numbers. The state doc is `../CONTEXT.md`.

## `A2 open gap research/` (2026-09-30)

A parallel web-research pass aimed at the ASE A2 exam, prioritized by the official 2026 task-list weights (Area A 58%, B 27%, C 15%).

Learner profile:
- GM ASEP student, about 2 years at a GM dealership, 7 ASE passes;
- confident in electrical, so basics are kept light;
- ASE rewards textbook and service-manual answers over shop habit.

**How to use it:**
1. **Read `conflict_resolution.md` first.** It checks 38 conflicts across the files, gives verdicts and the most likely ASE answer, and corrects 5 claims that contradict standard theory.
2. **Use task numbers from `../research.md`**, not from the individual files. `hardest_topics_and_strategy.md` and a few tags elsewhere use older or wrong numbering.
3. **Treat every finding as snippet-level.** The session's network policy blocked full-page fetches, so "DOCUMENTED (snippet)" means the claim appeared in a search-result excerpt for that URL. Verify against the full page or SI before a guide quotes it, and never print a number that isn't marked verified.
4. **Keep GM-specific facts labeled.** ASE is brand-neutral, so a GM example illustrates a general principle and isn't the rule itself.

**Tags:**
- DOCUMENTED (seen in a source), INFERRED (reasoned from sources), OPEN (unresolved), ANECDOTAL (forum or owner reports).
- Each finding also has its URL, access date, scope (universal / OEM-specific / needs SI) and A2 task number.
