The week

The technical report is always one of the cars in the week. New week keeps it and moves it to a random day; New day rebuilds just that day (the report car stays if it was there); New report swaps it for a fresh one. No concern shows up more than 5 times in a week. Warranty means the factory warranty (or a service contract) would pay for that concern. No causes on purpose: diagnosing is your job. Counts and odds are educated guesses. A student-made practice tool; not affiliated with or endorsed by GM.
