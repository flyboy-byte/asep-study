# Unit 3 Cram Sheet — Drive Axles

**Version 2 · Updated September 18, 2026**

## Noise and road test

- **Gear noise:** whine/howl; often changes with drive versus coast.
- **Bearing noise:** rough growl/rumble/grating; may pulse “wow-wow.”
- **Pinion bearing:** faster-pitched growl; pitch rises quickly; usually less load-sensitive than gear whine.
- **Carrier bearing:** slower growl/rumble; load may change it; may quiet with little load on a lift.
- **Axle/wheel bearing:** road-speed growl that can change during a controlled swerve.
- **Tire noise:** can mimic bearings; road surface, tire rotation, visible wear, and lift behavior help separate it.
- **Clunk:** excess driveline slack during torque reversal.
- **LSD chatter in turns:** check correct lubricant/friction treatment and clutch condition.
- **Changes with road surface:** tires.
- **Changes while cornering:** bearings or differential components.
- **Drive:** engine drives vehicle; drive side loaded.
- **Coast:** vehicle drives engine; coast side loaded.
- **Float:** little tooth load; lash split between sides.
- **Cruise:** steady road load.

## Differential types

- **Open, straight:** spiders travel with case but do not spin on their shaft.
- **Open, turn/slip:** spider rotation permits different side-gear speeds.
- **Brake traction control:** ABS brake action creates reaction torque; carrier stays mechanically open.
- **Clutch LSD/Posi:** side-gear thrust loads clutch packs; resists differentiation.
- **Helical/Torsen LSD:** gear thrust/friction biases torque; **not a full locker**.
- **GM/Eaton mechanical locker:** wheel-speed difference → governor/flyweight latch → cam ramps → clutches apply → full low-speed lock; lockout above roughly 20 mph.
- **Eaton ELocker:** commanded electromagnetic lock. Air lockers use pneumatic actuation; locking members vary.
- **Detroit Locker:** automatic locker; do not borrow the GM/Eaton engagement sequence.

## Axle support

- **Semi-floating:** axle shaft carries **torque + vehicle load**.
- **Full-floating:** axle shaft carries **mainly torque**; hub bearings carry vehicle load into housing.
- Semi-float retention may use inner C-lock or outer bearing retainer.

## Hypoid and tooth terms

- Hypoid pinion is **below ring-gear centerline** in the class example.
- Lowers driveshaft, permits stronger/larger pinion, creates sliding action, needs specified lubricant.
- Ring and pinion are **lapped/matched**; replace as a set.
- **Heel = outer. Toe = inner.**
- **Face = above pitch line. Flank = below.**
- Class wording: **drive = convex; coast = concave.**
- Drive condition: backlash at coast side.
- Coast condition: backlash at drive side.

## Hunting relationships

- **Hunting:** fraction cannot reduce; each pinion tooth eventually contacts every ring tooth; no timing marks. Example `41/11`.
- **Non-hunting:** reduces to whole number; repeating tooth subset; timing marks. Example `39/13 = 3`.
- **Partially non-hunting:** reducible but not whole; timing marks. Example `42/12 = 7/2`.

## Reverse cut

- Opposite tooth spiral from standard set.
- Front-axle application keeps forward drive on intended strong side.
- Can place pinion above centerline and reduce front driveshaft angle.
- Requires matching housing/oiling design.

## Setup

- **Backlash = gear-tooth clearance.**
- **Preload = initial bearing load.**
- Pinion preload checked by **rotating torque**.
- High tightening torque collapses the sleeve; it is not the preload reading.
- Crush sleeve is one-time use when setup must be repeated.
- Fluid level may be below fill-hole edge; use specification.

## Carrier and sensor notes

- **Removable/banjo carrier:** center section removes.
- **Integral/Salisbury carrier:** rear cover removes.
- Carrier uses tapered roller side/carrier bearings.
- Magnetic ABS sensors can collect metal debris; axle-end sensors can be affected by bearing wear.

## Fourteen instant answers

1. Whine? **Gears.**
2. Growl? **Bearings.**
3. Float? **Minimal tooth load.**
4. Semi-float carries? **Torque and weight.**
5. Full-float carries? **Torque; hub carries weight.**
6. Heel? **Outer.**
7. Toe? **Inner.**
8. Drive side? **Convex.**
9. Coast side? **Concave.**
10. Pinion preload checked with? **Rotating torque.**
11. Clutch LSD apply force? **Side-gear separating thrust.**
12. Helical LSD equal full locker? **No; it torque-biases.**
13. GM/Eaton trigger? **Wheel-speed difference moves the governor/flyweight.**
14. ELocker actuator? **Electromagnet.**
